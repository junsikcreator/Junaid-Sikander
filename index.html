Why Your Credit Card Is Actually a Financial Weapon

Most people treat their credit card like a liability. They swipe, they panic, they pay the minimum — and wonder why they are always broke. But here is what the banks do not advertise: used correctly, a credit card is one of the most powerful financial tools you will ever hold.

Video Editing — Strategy

Short-Form Editing Framework for YouTube Shorts

A pacing and structure framework for editing short-form content that retains viewers past the 15-second drop-off point.

The 3-second rule: First cut must happen before second 3. No exceptions. Static openers lose 40% of viewers before the hook lands.

Pacing structure for 60-second shorts:
0-3s: Visual hook — something unexpected on screen
3-8s: Audio hook — the most interesting line of the script
8-45s: Core content — one cut every 2-4 seconds
45-60s: Payoff + loop trigger — end on something that makes them rewatch

Sound design: Subtle background music at -18db, sound effects on every cut, silence used deliberately for emphasis — not by accident.

Caption style: Single word highlighted per beat, high contrast, positioned at 60% from top — avoids both the profile picture and the comment bar.
